# Gardun Map Contract

## North-to-south flow

```text
Mountain Fort
    |
West Ridge -- Old Gardun -- East Power Station
    |             |                  |
    +-------- Gardun Citadel --------+
                  |
         Government Quarter
                  |
Airfield ---- Dry Riverbed ---- Industrial Ward
                  |
              South Gate
```

## Stronghold Assault phases

1. Open the city: Airfield or Industrial Depot
2. Break communications: West Ridge and Government Relay
3. Control Old Gardun
4. Neutralize Mountain Fort and East Power
5. Assault the Citadel

## Route requirements

Every objective provides a main route, flank, interior route, elevated route, and conditional route where practical. Heavy vehicles remain outside dense inner-city and tunnel areas.
